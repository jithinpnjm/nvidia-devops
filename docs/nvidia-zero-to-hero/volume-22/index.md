## Overview

Volume 22 translates NVIDIA GPU infrastructure expertise into industry-specific solutions. Eight industry chapters, each covering:
- Typical workloads and performance targets
- Infrastructure constraints (latency, throughput, cost, compliance)
- Deployment patterns (training, inference, real-time, batch)
- Case studies and lessons learned

Plus one consulting meta-chapter on how to approach new customer engagements.

## Chapters

### Chapter 1: Consulting Methodology for Customer Engagement
Framework for understanding customer requirements, translating to infrastructure, designing solutions, and validating success.

### Chapter 2: Banking and Financial Services
Risk modeling, fraud detection, algorithmic trading. Low-latency inference, regulatory compliance, data privacy.

### Chapter 3: Generative AI and Large Language Models
Training and serving LLMs at scale. Model parallelism, inference optimization, multi-tenant serving.

### Chapter 4: Automotive and Autonomous Vehicles
Real-time inference for autonomous driving. Edge deployment, safety requirements, continuous learning pipelines.

### Chapter 5: Pharmaceuticals and Drug Discovery
Molecular simulation, protein folding, drug screening. High-precision compute, compliance with research standards.

### Chapter 6: Telecommunications
Network optimization, anomaly detection, resource allocation. High throughput, real-time response, 24/7 availability.

### Chapter 7: Healthcare and Medical Imaging
Medical image analysis, diagnostic AI, patient risk prediction. Compliance (HIPAA), data privacy, inference latency targets.

### Chapter 8: Manufacturing and Predictive Maintenance
Defect detection, equipment health monitoring, production optimization. Edge inference, cost sensitivity, uptime requirements.

### Chapter 9: Scientific Research and Simulation
Climate modeling, physics simulation, computational chemistry. Compute-intensive, long-running jobs, data management at scale.
